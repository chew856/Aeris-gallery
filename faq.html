The fine print

Questions, answered.

Everything worth knowing before the night. Anything else, write to us.

Booking & payment

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend four to six weeks ahead, especially for weddings and peak season. Booths are subject to availability, but some clients have booked as little as two days before their event, so it is always worth asking.

Is the deposit refundable?

Deposits are non-refundable, but they can be transferred to a new date with notice, depending on availability.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. We can split payment across two or three milestones.

On the day

Is set-up and teardown part of my booking time?

No. Set-up and teardown are separate from your service time and included in your rental price. We usually arrive two hours before your start time and pack down in 30 to 45 minutes. Early arrival is available for an added idle-time fee. For brand activations, up to three hours of set-up is included, and prior-day load-ins or next-day load-outs can be arranged.

Can we extend the booth on the night?

Yes. Add as much time as you like, as long as it is available.

Are you insured?

Aeris Gallery is fully insured. Our certificate of insurance is available on request.

Your venue

How much space does the booth need?

An area of at least 8 × 8 ft, within 15 ft of a power outlet.

Photos & sharing

How do we receive our photos?

We send a link to your private online gallery within 24 to 48 hours, with every photo, GIF and video from the night.

Can the booth make boomerangs or video?

Yes. Our set-ups capture photos, GIFs, boomerangs and short videos, made for sharing.

Do you offer prints?

Yes. Choose digital only, or add on-site prints in packages of 150 or 300.
