FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything people ask before they book. Can't find yours? Call (204) 990-6530.

Booking & payment

How far ahead should I book?

Four to six weeks for weddings and peak season. Booths go by availability, and we have booked events as little as two days out, so it is always worth asking.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the booth, the hours and the extras you choose. Send us your date and details and we will reply with a free quote.

Is the deposit refundable?

Deposits are non-refundable, but they can move to a new date with notice, depending on availability.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. You can split payment across two or three instalments.

On the day

Is set-up included in my hours?

No, it is on us. We arrive two hours before your start time and pack down in 30 to 45 minutes after. Set-up and teardown are included in your price. For brand activations, up to three hours of set-up is included.

Can we add time on the night?

Yes. Add as much time as you like, as long as the booth is free.

Are you insured?

Yes, Aeris Gallery is fully insured. We can send your venue our certificate of insurance.

How much space do you need?

At least 8 × 8 ft, within 15 ft of a power outlet.

Photos & sharing

How do guests get their photos?

Instantly. Guests get their photos sent straight to their phone right after they take them. You also get a private online gallery with every photo, GIF and video within 24 hours.

Can the booth do GIFs, boomerangs and video?

Yes. Our booths capture photos, GIFs, boomerangs and short videos, made for sharing.

Do you offer prints?

Yes. Add on-site prints in packages of 150 or 300.

Can we add a custom LED sign?

Yes. Choose one of our preset signs or order a custom one with your names, a hashtag or your logo.

Still deciding?

Send us your date. We'll reply with availability and a free quote, and help you pick the right booth.
