The Glam Booth

Simplicity that speaks volumes. Studio portraits, in black and white, for every guest.

Words: Aeris Gallery
Setting: Winnipeg
For: Weddings, brands,
private events

The Glam Booth is a timeless take on modern photography, made for people who appreciate elegance in its purest form.

Every portrait is black and white with a retro-modern twist. A clean backdrop, soft white light and refined editing come together to make photographs that feel effortless and iconic at once, the kind of image that looks as good on a mantel in thirty years as it does on a phone tonight.

Guests step in and, for a few frames, become the centrepiece of a classic editorial shoot. Every detail is designed with intention, from the polished set-up to the finish on each photograph.

Perfect for weddings, corporate events and private gatherings. The result is more than a picture. It is a keepsake that captures beauty, confidence and style in its most authentic form.

“Effortless. Bold.
Unforgettable.”

The detailsWhat every booking includes
Space

Signage
Add a preset LED sign, or commission a custom one.
Finish
Black and white, studio lighting, refined editing on every frame.
