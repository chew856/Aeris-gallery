Editor's letterAeris Gallery, Winnipeg

The first of its kind in Canada.

Aeris Gallery is Winnipeg's premier photo booth house, built around one idea: your guests deserve to be photographed the way magazines photograph people.

So we built Canada's first fully mirrored booth, a walk-in cabin of infinite reflection that turns every guest into the centre of their own editorial. Then we built its opposite.

The Glam Booth trades colour for black and white, blending retro charm with modern luxury. Soft white light, a clean backdrop and a studio-grade lens produce portraits that feel timeless the moment they print.

From private dinners to grand celebrations, your guests leave with keepsakes worth framing, and a night they will be talking about long after it ends. We are starting in Winnipeg. We do not plan to stop there.

With love, AerisFounder's note
