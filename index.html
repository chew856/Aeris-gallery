Good to know.

How far ahead should I book?

Four to six weeks for weddings and peak season. Booths go by availability, and we have booked events as little as two days out, so it is always worth asking.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the booth, the hours and the extras you choose. Send us your date and details and we will reply with a free quote.

Is set-up included in my hours?

No, it is on us. We arrive two hours before your start time and pack down in 30 to 45 minutes after. Set-up and teardown are included in your price. For brand activations, up to three hours of set-up is included.

How much space do you need?

At least 8 × 8 ft, within 15 ft of a power outlet.

How do guests get their photos?

Instantly. Guests get their photos sent straight to their phone right after they take them. You also get a private online gallery with every photo, GIF and video within 24 hours.
