The Mirrored Booth

A walk-in cabin of infinite reflection, and the first of its kind in Canada.

Words: Aeris Gallery
Setting: Winnipeg
For: Weddings, brands,
private events

Step through the door and the room disappears. Walls lined with mirror throw every light, every balloon and every pose back at you, again and again, until the cabin feels the size of a ballroom.

It is part installation, part photo studio. Modern design meets captivating visual effects, and guests stop posing for the camera and start playing with the space: leaning into the reflections, finding the angle where they repeat forever, filming the moment the lights change.

Lighting is customised to your event, so the booth wears your colours. It photographs, records GIFs, boomerangs and short video, and every frame is built to be shared. At a wedding it becomes the room everyone disappears into. At a brand launch it becomes the content.

A mirrored booth in Winnipeg is more than a photo opportunity. It is the part of the night your guests describe to the people who were not there.

“Every pose,
repeated into infinity.”
