Privacy

Your details, kept close.

What we collect

When you request a booking we receive your name, email address, phone number and the event details you choose to share. When you join The Gallery Letter we receive your email address.

Why we collect it

To reply to your request, quote and plan your event, and, if you subscribed, to send occasional news. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

Event photographs

Photos taken in our booths are delivered to the event host through a private gallery. We will only use images in our own marketing with the host's permission.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from The Gallery Letter at any time, or ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, by emailing aerisgallery@outlook.com.
