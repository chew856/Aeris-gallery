Privacy

Your details, kept close.

What we collect

When you check a date or request a quote, we receive the details you enter: your name, email, phone number and information about your event.

Why we collect it

Only to reply to your request, send a quote and plan your event. We do not sell or share your information.

On this device

If you start the booking form and leave, your answers are saved in your own browser so you don't have to start again. They are cleared when you send the form.

Event photographs

Photos taken in our booths are delivered to the event host in a private gallery. We only use images in our own marketing with the host's permission.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, email aerisgallery@outlook.com.
